Install

  1. Download the installer from the latest release.
  2. Close Premiere Pro and After Effects, run the installer and click Next until it finishes.
  3. If Windows asks about the firewall, choose Allow. Without this the phone cannot connect.
  4. Open Premiere Pro or After Effects. Go to Window > Extensions > Pocket Monitor.

Turn on the engine

This tells your Adobe app to send its picture to Pocket Monitor. You do it once.

Premiere Pro

Edit > Preferences > Playback, then tick Pocket Monitor under Video Device.

After Effects

Edit > Preferences > Video Preview, then choose Pocket Monitor as the output device.

The panel shows a green tick when the engine is on.

Connect your phone

  1. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the computer.
  2. Point the phone camera at the QR code in the panel and open the link.
  3. Or open the address shown under the QR code, and type the 4 letter code.

Sound

In Premiere Pro, leave Audio Device on your speakers. If Pocket Monitor is listed for audio, tick it too. On the phone, tap Sound to hear your edit. Phones keep sound off until you tap, so it is never a surprise.

Overlays

On the phone, tap Overlays to add guides, safe zones, and frames for common social formats. They show on the phone only. They are never added to your project.

Troubleshooting

The phone cannot open the page

Check that the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, not mobile data and not a guest network. Some routers block devices from talking to each other (AP isolation). Try the phone's hotspot: join it from the computer, then scan again.

Windows Firewall blocked it

Open Windows Security > Firewall & network protection > Allow an app through firewall. Tick PocketMonitorHelper for Private networks. Or run the installer again.

Different Wi-Fi names, or more than one connection

Use the Connection list in the panel. Choose the one that matches the network your phone is on, then scan the new QR code.

The panel says the helper is not running

Click Start helper in the panel. If it still fails, start Pocket Monitor from the Start menu and wait a few seconds.

The picture is black or the panel says the engine is off

Redo Turn on the engine. In Premiere Pro, restart playback after ticking it. In the panel menu (the three dots) choose Show guide again for the steps.

I need to send a report

Click Send report in the panel. It saves a small file on your computer with settings and recent messages. Nothing is uploaded. Attach the file to a GitHub issue if you want help.

Uninstall

Windows Settings > Apps > Pocket Monitor > Uninstall. This removes the panel, the engine plugin and the helper.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your network. The picture and sound go from your computer straight to your phone over your own Wi-Fi. There is no account, no tracking and no cloud. The panel checks GitHub once a day to see if a new version exists, and sends nothing about you.

Before you judge colour

  • Turn off True Tone, Night Shift (or any blue-light filter) and auto-brightness.
  • Pick a fixed brightness and compare in the same lighting every time.
  • This is a phone screen, not a calibrated print proof. Fabric and printer still affect the final colour.
  • Android browsers and vendor display modes vary more than iPhone. Check important colours on the real device.

The viewer applies no filters and no colour tint of its own.
